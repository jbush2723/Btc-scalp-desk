How the prices are calculated

Entry uses the current ask on the signal side. Planned price risk is the largest of 2¢, twice the spread, or half the recent midpoint range. The stop rounds down to a published price increment; the target rounds up to at least twice that risk above entry. No entry is shown if the target exceeds the tradable price ceiling, planned price risk exceeds 10¢, or supplied costs leave less than 1.5:1 estimated net reward-to-risk. Targets are scenarios, not forecasts. Check your actual Kalshi fees; no fee rate is assumed.

UP = YES scalp bias · DOWN = NO scalp bias · WAIT = no confirmed setup. Trend describes the Kalshi contract, not official BTC/BRTI movement. These rules have not been backtested and do not estimate win probability or profitability after fees.

What makes the signal change?

UP requires the YES midpoint to rise at least 1¢ over about 30 seconds, with at least 60% YES taker volume over 10 seconds, 55% over 30 seconds and 50% over 60 seconds. DOWN uses the same thresholds for NO buying and a falling YES midpoint. The setup must hold across at least 3 fresh snapshots spanning 4 seconds. WAIT applies to conflicts, stale or incomplete data, spreads above 3¢, fewer than 5 trades or 20 contracts in 10 seconds, more than 10% unknown taker volume, or the final minute. History resets at contract rollover or after a quote gap.
